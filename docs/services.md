# Services Catalog

JUJU Films is a modern creator collective that builds original stories. Our divisions are structured to support brands, streaming platforms, and creators.

---

## 1. Vertical Micro-Drama Production (VMD)
- **Format**: 9:16 mobile-optimized video, 1 to 5 minutes per episode.
- **Location Scope**: Production units based in Mumbai and Delhi NCR, India.
- **Framework**: Implements the *JUJU VMD Framework™* to optimize watch time, episode cliffhangers, and subscriber conversion rates.
- **Best For**: D2C brands, streaming platforms launched for short-form dramas, and creator-led networks.

---

## 2. Original Series & Features (JUJU Storytellers)
- **Format**: Premium landscape content (OTT platforms, feature films).
- **Strategy**: Focus on character development, cultural impact, and intellectual property value. We partner with top writers and showrunners to develop series from concept to delivery.
- **Best For**: Major streaming networks (Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar, JioCinema).

---

## 3. Brand-Integrated Commercials (JUJU Commercials)
- **Format**: Standard commercials (15s to 60s) and long-form brand stories.
- **Philosophy**: Organic integration where the brand is built into the narrative world. We capture target attention by funding cultural stories instead of traditional ad placements.
- **Best For**: Brands shifting from high-frequency ad impressions to deep attention models.

---

## 4. JUJU AI Lab
- **Format**: Pre-production visualization, automated localized translation, and scaling pipelines.
- **Scope**: AI assists scriptwriters, artists, and editors with concept boards and localization, accelerating development by 3x while retaining human creative direction.
- **Best For**: Multi-regional campaigns and fast-turnaround content testing.
