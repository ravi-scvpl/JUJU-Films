# JUJU Films

> We build stories. Brands fund culture.

JUJU Films is a creator collective making micro-dramas, series, and films—where brands don’t interrupt stories, they enable them. Not a production house, but a creator collective. JUJU partners with India’s best storytellers to build original IP, funded by brands that believe in culture over clutter.

## Our Core Offerings

### 1. Vertical Micro-Dramas (VMD)
Short, bingeable emotional stories in 9:16 vertical format designed for mobile attention spans. JUJU operates as a Vertical Micro Drama Production House based in Mumbai and Delhi NCR, India, delivering end-to-end VMD creation (pilots, series, and episodic branded entertainment) optimized for platforms like Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and emerging drama apps.
- **Learn more**: [Vertical Micro Dramas](/vertical-micro-drama-production-india)
- **Our framework**: [VMD Playbook](/vertical-micro-drama-production-india/playbook)

### 2. Original Series & Films
Limited series and features with long shelf life and strong cultural recall.

### 3. Brand-Enabled Content
Stories where the brand lives inside the world—silently, authentically. High attention, not high frequency. Cultural presence, not placement.

### 4. JUJU AI Lab
AI-Powered Storytelling. Faster creation, regional scale, future-ready formats. At JUJU, AI doesn’t replace emotion—it removes friction. We use AI for script exploration, pre-visualisation, and regional adaptations.
