# JUJU VMD Framework™ & Knowledge Base

This document outlines the core intellectual property, storytelling theories, and corporate guidelines behind JUJU Films.

---

## 1. The JUJU VMD Framework™
Vertical Micro-Dramas (VMD) succeed not because attention spans are short, but because stories are built around audience retention and mobile viewing habits. The framework comprises 5 core layers:

| Layer | Component | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **Layer 1** | **Hook** | Grab viewer attention in the first 3 seconds of scrolling. Must introduce an immediate, compelling visual or narrative conflict. |
| **Layer 2** | **Character** | Build emotional attachment. Viewers return because they care about the characters, not just the plot. |
| **Layer 3** | **Escalation** | Ensure constant narrative progression. A relationship shifts, a secret is revealed, or a conflict rises mid-episode. |
| **Layer 4** | **Cliffhanger** | End each episode with an unanswered question or high-tension moment to drive anticipation for the next episode. |
| **Layer 5** | **Call-to-Action** | Make it frictionless for the user to follow, subscribe, or tap to watch the next episode. |

## 2. Business Philosophy
- **"We build stories. Brands fund culture."**: Brands should participate organically in narrative worlds. If the brand can be completely deleted from the script without changing the story, the integration is too shallow.
- **Creator Collective over Production House**: JUJU operates on a co-ownership model. We partner directly with storytellers, giving them long-term equity and ownership in the intellectual property we construct together.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: Where are Vertical Micro-Dramas distributed?
VMDs are designed for social platforms (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok) and dedicated short-form drama applications (e.g., ReelShort, DramaBox) as well as OTT platforms.

#### Q: How does the JUJU AI Lab work?
We integrate generative AI tools (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, language models) strictly in pre-production. This assists in concept sketching, storyboard visualization, script exploration, and localized dubbing/scaling, ensuring the core storytelling remains uniquely human-driven.

#### Q: How do brands measure success with JUJU Films?
Unlike standard ads that optimize for high impressions but low attention, JUJU measures success by watch time, return rate (percentage of users binging multiple episodes), and long-term brand recall.
